# Planet photos

*[Polska wersja](README_pl.md)*

Photos that [`tools/board_svg.py`](../../tools/README.md#board-generator) places in the station spaces beside the board. All of them come from NASA and are in the public domain. Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to a square with the planet filling the frame, and scaled down.

| File | Photo | Author | Source |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| [earth.jpg](earth.jpg) | "The Blue Marble" – the Earth from about 29,400 km, 7 December 1972 | NASA / Apollo 17 crew | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Earth_seen_from_Apollo_17.jpg) |
| [mars.jpg](mars.jpg) | Mosaic of 102 Viking 1 Orbiter images with Valles Marineris at the centre, 22 February 1980 | NASA / USGS | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mars_Valles_Marineris.jpeg) |
| [ceres.jpg](ceres.jpg) | PIA19562 – Ceres seen by the Dawn spacecraft from 13,600 km, 6 May 2015 | NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PIA19562-Ceres-DwarfPlanet-Dawn-RC3-image19-20150506.jpg), [NASA Photojournal](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA19562) |
| [vesta.jpg](vesta.jpg) | Full view of Vesta – mosaic of Dawn images (2011–2012) | NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCAL/MPS/DLR/IDA | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vesta_full_mosaic.jpg) |

The Mars mosaic has a dark gap in the bottom right corner where images are missing. Vesta is not round, so the circular mask on the board trims its sides slightly.

Without these files the generator draws the planets as plain-coloured discs.
